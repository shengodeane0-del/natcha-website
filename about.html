About Us

Culturally grounded disability support services

About Natcha NDIS

We chose the name Natcha Tong Nong Ga, meaning healing spirit through mother, to honour the role of mother earth and community in restoring balance, stability and social and spiritual wellbeing.

We know support is not only about care, but about dignity, connection and growth. Alongside traditional disability support services, we offer clients access to meaningful cultural connection and social activities including:

  • On Country activities and excursions
  • Cultural learning, artefact making and cultural connection groups
  • Meaningful therapeutic and cultural supports

We are Aboriginal owned and led, and we support participants from every background. Culture shapes how we work, not who is welcome.

Our Values

Our values guide how we show up each day with respect, honesty and responsibility to community. With a focus on meaningful change for clients, building stability, confidence and independence over time, we are guided by our values:

Respect, dignity and self determination

Cultural safety and community connection

Accountability and honesty

Growth and independence

Our Team

Our diverse team brings together experienced, qualified support workers and practitioners who understand the importance of trust, boundaries and consistency.

We prioritise relationship based support and small, stable teams so participants know who is walking beside them. Our team expertise includes:

  • Cultural knowledge and mentoring
  • Trauma informed practice and care
  • Collaborative support planning
  • Life transition support and planning

Community and cultural support

We support participants to build confidence and connection in their community. This includes social participation, mentoring and culturally grounded activities that strengthen identity and belonging.

Our approach supports psychosocial wellbeing through relationship and safe exposure to community settings.

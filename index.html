Our purpose

Culture is how we work, not who we work with

Natcha Tong Nong Ga means healing spirit through mother. We are Aboriginal owned and led, and the way we work comes from that: relationship before paperwork, consistency over churn, and support that respects who someone is.

We support participants from every background. Culturally grounded practice is not a restriction on who can come to us. It is the reason our supports hold when others have not.

We specialise in psychosocial disability and complex trauma histories, building long term pathways to stability and wellbeing.

What we do

Four ways we work alongside people

Flexible, in line with your plan, with a minimum three hour service.

Daily living and personal support

Personal care, household help and skill building, so home stays steady and independence grows.

  • Personal care and hygiene
  • Household tasks and meals
  • Transport and appointments
  • Independent living skills

Therapeutic and psychosocial care

Trauma informed support for emotional regulation, behaviour and functional capacity.

  • Allied health, OT, psychology, speech
  • Behaviour support planning
  • Emotional regulation
  • Functional capacity assessment

Community and cultural connection

On Country activities and connection days that build confidence and belonging at your own pace.

  • Community access
  • Cultural learning and group days
  • On Country excursions
  • Culturally grounded respite

Support coordination and transitions

Advocacy and practical implementation, including complex cases and transitions out of custody.

  • Plan implementation
  • Crisis and transition support
  • Justice transition planning
  • ILO pathway development

Support coordination

A plan on paper is not support

Our coordinators do the calls, the bookings and the follow up, so funded supports turn into services that actually run. We take complex cases, including transitions out of custody and into stable housing.

Referrals come from participants, families, planners and partner organisations. Any plan type, NDIA managed, plan managed or self managed. We come back within two business days.

Plan implementation

Turning funded supports into booked, delivered services with the right providers.

Crisis and transition

Hands on coordination when housing, health or safety changes suddenly.

Justice transition planning

Supports organised before release, so the first week out is not the hardest.

ILO pathways

Individualised Living Options explored, designed and put to the NDIA.

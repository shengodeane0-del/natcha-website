Design direction

Natcha NDIS

Colour and structure drawn from the mark itself. Black, red and ochre are already the brand, they have just never been used on the site.

Colour

Every colour below is lifted straight out of the logo. The roles matter more than the colours: red is a highlight, never a background, because a page of red reads as a warning.

Ink

#0D0D0D

Page ground, headers, footers. The dominant colour.

Ochre

#D7741D

Every button and every link. Black text on it, always.

Red

#ED1C24

Accent only. Rules, underlines, the odd marker. Never a block.

Paper

#FAF8F4

Light sections, so the page is not black end to end.

Sand

#EFE9DF

Quiet bands and cards on the light sections.

Slate

#171717

Cards sitting on Ink, so panels separate without a border.

One rule worth holding. Red on a light background fails readability at small sizes. Where red text is needed on Paper or Sand, it darkens to #C2151C. Ochre is never used as text on white.

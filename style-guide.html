Design direction

Natcha NDIS

Black and white, drawn from the mark itself. No colour on the page, so the photography and the words do the work.

Colour

Black, white and a short ramp of greys. The roles matter more than the values, because with no colour on the page contrast is the only signal left.

Ink

#0D0D0D

Page ground, headers, footers. The dominant colour.

White

#FAFAFA

Buttons and links on the dark sections. Black text on it, always.

Graphite

#5A5A5A

Secondary text on the light sections. Never for anything that has to be read at a glance.

Paper

#FAFAFA

Light sections, so the page is not black end to end.

Sand

#EFEFEF

Quiet bands and cards on the light sections.

Slate

#171717

Cards sitting on Ink, so panels separate without a border.

One rule worth holding. Buttons are white with black text on the dark sections, and black with white text on the light ones. Never white on white. If a button is hard to find, the ground is wrong, not the button.
